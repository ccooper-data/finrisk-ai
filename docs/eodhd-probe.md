# EODHD probe adapter

EODHD is integrated only as a probe candidate until empirical coverage is measured.

Current documentation states that delisted U.S. symbols can be listed and queried through the normal EOD endpoint, and that U.S. symbol-change history is available. EOD coverage for delisted securities predating 2018 may be price-only.

The generic security-master validity dates in this first adapter are placeholders for candidate discovery only; they are not accepted historical issuer/security links. FinRisk's separate security-resolution evidence and date-overlap rules remain authoritative.
