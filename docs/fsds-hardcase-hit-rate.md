# SEC filing-era hard-case hit rate

This one-off evidence job measures whether the bulk SEC Financial Statement Data Sets actually cover the difficult identity population before any further automation or market calls.

Population: first 100 distressed issuers without current tickers under the frozen historical-identity ordering.
Source window: 2009-2026 quarterly SUB records.
Primary result: unique CIK hit rate plus filing-era name row count.

A high hit rate validates SUB as the historical-name backbone. A low hit rate means the source is insufficient and should not be automated further.
