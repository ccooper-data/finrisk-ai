# Entity continuity contract

CIK permanence identifies an SEC registrant; it does not prove economic continuity across bankruptcy, merger, reorganization, spinoff or successor formation.

FinRisk models cross-CIK continuity explicitly. Supported relations include predecessor/successor, merger survivor, bankruptcy successor and spinoff. Every cross-entity relation requires an effective date and source provenance.

No model feature or historical security link may traverse from one CIK to another merely because names, tickers or assets appear related.
