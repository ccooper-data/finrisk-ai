# Historical provider probe plan

Before paying for or ingesting a full historical market universe, FinRisk can test candidate providers on a deterministic probe cohort.

The probe prioritizes distressed issuers lacking current tickers—the exact population most likely to expose survivorship limitations—plus a non-distressed comparison group. Provider capability claims can therefore be tested empirically before a large ingestion.

Probe success does not replace full-cohort coverage evidence.
